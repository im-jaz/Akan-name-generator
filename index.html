The Story Behind Akan Names

Akan names are deeply rooted in Ghanaian culture and are traditionally given according to the day a child is born.

Origins

The Akan people are one of Ghana's largest ethnic groups. Their naming tradition has been passed down through generations and remains an important part of their culture.

Weekday Names

Every day of the week has both a male and female Akan name. Your birth day determines the traditional name you receive.

Cultural Identity

These names represent identity, belonging, and the continuation of a tradition that has connected families for centuries.
